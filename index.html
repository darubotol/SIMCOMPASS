DECISION SUPPORT

Awaiting price tests

Enter at least two price points and their expected sales rates to compare cash recovery, total contribution and sell-through time.
How the comparison works

Cash generation is price × units sold over time. Contribution profit is (selling price − unit cost) × units sold. Challenge-reserved stock is excluded from retail inventory. Retail boosts affect the sales-rate estimate only when the boost's verified game mechanic changes retail throughput.
